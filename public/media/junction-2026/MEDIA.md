# Junction 2026 photos for the portfolio

From Kirill, 19.11.2026. Every file is cleaned of metadata. Each photo comes in four versions: `name.jpg` (≤2400 px, JPEG fallback), `-full.webp` (≤2400 px), `-lg.webp` (≤1600 px), `-sm.webp` (≤800 px). Make AVIF yourself in the build pipeline.
Put them in the repo at `public/media/junction-2026/` (or wherever your stack expects) and commit this file next to them.

## Rights and consent (read before using)
- **Photos 02, 04, 06 look like the official Junction photographer's work** (Flickr-style original filenames). The original of a photo NOT included in this pack carries the metadata "Henrik Hiilamo", so that's likely the same photographer. Before publishing, find these photos on Junction's official Flickr, check the license and credit them exactly as the license requires (e.g. "Photo: Junction / Henrik Hiilamo"). If the license doesn't allow it, don't use them. Ask Kirill.
- **01 and 02 show teammates** (Valtteri, Temuulen, Justinas). They agreed to GitHub links, but agreeing to a photo on the site is a separate question. Put it in the list of questions for Kirill.
- **06 shows strangers' faces in close-up.** Don't use it as a large image.
- One photo is deliberately left out of the pack: a minister of Finland speaking on stage. A portfolio must not suggest any connection to a politician.

## Palette straight from the photos (suggested; can serve as the basis for the site's colours)
- Base: almost black `#090709`
- Violet light: `#8823b5`
- Warm coral/amber light sticks: `#e0685b`
- Magenta: `#cf2672`
The light sticks crossing in the frame (violet and orange) are a ready-made visual motif: they can flow like liquid neon.
