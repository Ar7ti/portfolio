Case 02 · Roblox · Solo · May–Nov 2026

Sky Island
Tower Defense

A co-op tower defense on Roblox. Up to 4 players hold a floating island against 15 waves and a boss.

Live

published on Roblox, 25 Oct 2026

223

commits, all mineas of 18 Nov 2026

1.1

Ember Update: new map, new season

My part.Everything: game design, all the Luau code, the UI in React-lua.

Hardest call.The UI is more than half the game: ~23K lines of React-lua, touched in 96 of 223 commits. In July I dropped plain Roblox GUIs and rebuilt it in React-lua, so new screens like summons and rewards could keep coming.

Proof it works.My Python scripts drive Roblox Studio: they play a match, try bad placements, check phone layouts and measure frame times.

Next time.Get real players testing before launch, not after.

Code.Waves, enemy movement and the UI are public. Combat and economy stay private, so cheaters can't read them.
